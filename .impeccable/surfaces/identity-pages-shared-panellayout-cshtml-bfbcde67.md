Scope: Admin and public shell, Identity access and existing CRUD views; mode Operate. Administrator needs reliable, rapid access to Clientes, Proveedores, Productos, Cotizaciones and Usuarios. Public registrants can sign in but cannot operate modules.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A workshop's indexed record system replaces the stock ASP.NET template, not a decorative printing-press hero. Every route remains recognizable by its section tab and record heading.
OWN-WORLD: Warm white sheets, deep ink typography and a single mineral teal action color with a restrained rust marker. Strong registration rules, folio numbers and tab silhouettes carry hierarchy; no simulated metrics, photos or invented transactions. Original geometric imprint mark accompanies the name.
STORY: The home introduces four real modules. Admin enters a panel with four actionable indexed records, opens a list, edits or returns; unprivileged registrant sees a clear permissions explanation. CRUD tables and forms inherit the same language.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact top masthead with imprint at left and account on right; a dark narrow section rail establishes location; a generous white working sheet shows the current module, its primary action, and the first records without requiring scroll on desktop. On mobile the rail becomes a labeled toggle above the sheet.
FORM: Archive/index cards (assigned candidate 3, seed cbdf6dad). A stable masthead, left index and repeated folio labels work on sparse or dense records; keyboard focus and validation remain visually explicit.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
